Privacy Policy

Effective 18 September 2026

This policy explains what WETHEFIT collects when you use the app, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. WETHEFIT is operated by Faisal Khani ("WETHEFIT", "we", "us"), based in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

1. What we collect

InformationWhere it comes fromWhy we use it
Email address and passwordYou, at sign-upTo create and secure your account and let you sign in. Passwords are stored only in hashed form.
Name, headline, profile choices (public or private account, distance unit)YouTo show your profile to other members according to your privacy choice.
Sports, teams and players you followYouTo show news headlines about them in your feed.
Posts, workouts, articles, events, comments, likes, attendance, followsYouTo run the community feed and show your activity to members allowed to see it.

2. Who can see what

  • Public account: your profile and posts are visible to any signed-in member.
  • Private account: your profile and posts are visible only to your connections. Trainer pages are always visible so members can find a coach.
  • Messages: only you and the other person. Members can message their connections; anyone can message a trainer.
  • Reviews: visible on the trainer's page with your name.

3. Services we rely on

We use trusted providers to run WETHEFIT. Each only receives what it needs for its job:

  • Supabase hosts our database, accounts and file storage. Data is stored in Supabase's Mumbai (India) region.
  • Expo (Expo Application Services) delivers push notifications and app updates. It receives your push token and the notification text.
  • Google News RSS provides headlines for the sports, teams and players you follow. Your phone requests those feeds directly, so Google receives those search terms and your IP address. Our privacy policy does not cover Google's handling of that request.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API), or Google Places if enabled in the future, provides places near you. Your phone sends your approximate location to run the search.
  • Apple and Google distribute the app and may process crash and usage data under their own policies.

4. How long we keep it

Your account data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, posts, comments, likes, connections, messages, reviews, push tokens and trainer profile are deleted from our database immediately. Backups are overwritten within 30 days. We may keep records of reports and enforcement actions, in a form that no longer identifies you where possible, to keep the community safe.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile, privacy setting, sports and trainer profile in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account from Profile & settings → Delete account, or by following the steps on the delete your account page.
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings; we stop sending them.
  • Location: decline or revoke the permission; the Nearby tab then shows a sample area.
  • Access or correction: email us and we will send you a copy of your data or correct it.

To exercise any of these, email support@wethefit.com from the address on your account.

6. Children

WETHEFIT accounts are for people aged 18 or over. Coaches for children are listed for parents and guardians, who hold the account and arrange sessions on the child's behalf. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is controlled row by row, so members can only read what they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will tell you as soon as we reasonably can.

8. International transfers

Our servers are in India, and our providers operate globally. By using WETHEFIT you understand that your information is processed outside the country where you live, under the protections described here and in our providers' terms.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
